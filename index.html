Four rooms. No waiting room.

Fatherhood is a shift that doesn't end. This is where dads sort the money, hold down the work, fix the house and get out on a Monday night. The talking happens on the way.

Pick your stage at the top. Every room then shows you the men at the same point as you: nappies, nursery runs, teenagers, or every other weekend.

Free. No real names needed. Nothing here is therapy, and nothing here is regulated financial advice. It is men who have been through it, and the tools they wished they'd had.

Why the front door says Money

Ask a man how he is and you get "fine". Ask him what child maintenance is going to cost him and you get the truth, the whole evening, and three other men joining in. The things that actually bring dads through a door are money, work and the house. So those are the rooms.

Fatherhood is not a room here. It is what you end up talking about in all of them.

How a stream works

Each room has a stream for each stage. It is a group chat, hosted somewhere ordinary, with a few rules: what is said there stays there, nobody has to fix anybody, and no selling. A stream opens once enough men at that stage ask for one, and it gets a man who has been through it to keep an eye.

Chat is the room for whatever is on your mind, and the one that also happens in person. Monday night, a back room, a pint or a brew. Bring a mate if you can't face walking in alone. Most of us couldn't.

If tonight is bad, the Samaritans answer on 116 123, free, any hour. If you are thinking about ending your life, call 999 or go to A&E. Then come back here tomorrow. We'll still be open.
