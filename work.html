Work

You are allowed to be a father at work. The law says so more than your boss does. Here is what you can actually ask for, and what to do when the job turns on you.

Leave you can take

  • Paternity leave: two weeks, taken in one block or two separate weeks, any time in the first year. Statutory pay is a flat weekly rate set each April. Check the figure on gov.uk, and check your contract, which may pay more.
  • Shared Parental Leave: up to 50 weeks of leave and 37 of pay shared between you. Awkward paperwork, real time with your child. Few men take it. The ones who do rarely regret it.
  • Unpaid parental leave: 18 weeks per child before they turn 18, in blocks of a week, up to four weeks a year. For school holidays, hospital stays, the bad patch.
  • Time off for dependants: unpaid, reasonable, when a child is ill or the childcare collapses that morning. You do not need permission in advance, you need to tell them as soon as you can.

Hours that fit

  • You can ask for flexible working from your first day in a job, twice a year. They must deal with the request within two months and can only refuse on one of eight business grounds, in writing.
  • Ask in writing, say "statutory flexible working request", and propose how it would work for them, not just for you. Compressed hours and a fixed early finish two days a week get agreed more often than full home working.
  • If it is refused, ask which of the eight grounds. Vague is not a ground.

When the job turns on you

  • Redundancy: after two years you are owed statutory redundancy pay, notice pay, and a fair process with consultation. Being picked because you took leave or asked for flexible hours is discrimination, and a tribunal claim has a three-month clock.
  • Being managed out: keep a diary. Dates, what was said, who was there. Nobody believes a feeling. Everyone believes a diary.
  • Acas helpline is free and knows the law better than your HR department. Your union, if you have one, is what you have been paying for.
  • Settlement agreements: the employer pays for you to get legal advice on it. Take the advice. Do not sign in the room.

Side income without the fantasy

  • The trading allowance lets you earn £1,000 a year on the side tax free with no return. Over that, register as self-employed by 5 October after the tax year it started.
  • Selling your own old stuff is not trading. Buying to sell is.
  • The side hustles that work for dads with no evenings are the ones that use what you already know: a trade, a spreadsheet, a language, a van.
